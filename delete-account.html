Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 16, 2026

You can permanently delete your Lastly account and all associated data directly from within the app, at any time, without contacting support.

Account deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no waiting period, and once deletion is complete, it cannot be undone or recovered.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Lastly app and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom tab bar).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm you want to permanently delete your account.
  5. Confirm a second time — this step exists to make sure deletion isn't triggered by accident, since it cannot be reversed.

If you're unable to access the app (for example, your device is lost or you've forgotten your password), you can request deletion by emailing hello@lastlyapp.com from the email address associated with your account.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All echoes you've recorded — audio, video, and text — including the underlying media files
  • Your Testament (Lastly Echo) setup, including check-in history and witness configuration
  • All recipients and their delivery information associated with your echoes
  • Chorus sessions you've organized and their contributor recordings
  • Your Circle (Heartbeats) setup and member list
  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number, timezone, push notification token)

What is not deleted

Two categories of data are not covered by in-app account deletion:

  • Chorus contributions from other people. If you organized a Chorus session, recordings contributed by other participants belong to those participants and are not deleted when you delete your own account.
  • Financial records. Apple and Google retain their own transaction and billing records for subscriptions independently of Lastly, in accordance with their own policies. Lastly does not separately retain payment card information — all billing is handled directly by the App Store or Google Play.

No additional retention period applies beyond what's described above — deletion of everything Lastly controls happens at the moment you confirm it in the app.
